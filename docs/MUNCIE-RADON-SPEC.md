**Content integrity rules for the writing pass:** every local claim checkable-true; answer-first paragraphs under every H2; the mined Reddit/PAA phrasing appears in FAQ questions verbatim-adjacent ("Is 3.3 pCi/L worth mitigating?"); zero SWAT-style census filler.

**Operator/legal note:** Indiana requires IDOH certification to perform radon testing/mitigation services. The site is a lead generation brand; leads route to a certified operator (the renter). Verify certification during buyer vetting — it's on the outreach shortlist criteria.
